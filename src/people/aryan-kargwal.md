Aryan Kargwal is a PhD student at Polytechnique Montréal, supervised by Prof. Pierre-Yves Lajoie. His research focuses on robot perception, planning, and formal verification for autonomous systems operating in dynamic environments.

Before beginning his PhD, Aryan completed a master’s degree in telecommunications at INRS. His thesis examined region-based segmentation of Arctic coastal imagery under difficult visual conditions. The work developed his background in computer vision, structured scene understanding, and perception under uncertainty.

## Current Work

Aryan studies how robots can interpret changing environments, build structured spatial representations, generate plans, and verify safety requirements before acting.

His current research interests include open-set perception, uncertainty-aware planning, runtime verification, safe robot navigation, and multi-robot autonomy in environments shared with people.
